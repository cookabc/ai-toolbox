The app icon is pixel art drawn in code, not generated from a prompt.

Source: AppIconArt in Sources/AIToolbox/PixelArt.swift — a 32 × 32 cell design
(a 16 × 16 variant for the 16-pixel slot): midnight-navy bands joined by dithered
seams, three shaded bars in cyan, blue and violet on a floor line, and a spark.
The same bars are the header mark (PixelArt.appMark) and the menu-bar template
(PixelArt.menuBarMark), so the three read as one product.

It is full-bleed and opaque on purpose: since macOS 26 the system masks such an
icon to its standard shape, while an icon with a transparent margin is shrunk onto
a grey plate.

Regenerate AppIcon.png and AppIcon.iconset/ (every slot drawn at a whole number of
pixels per cell) with:

    USAGE_RENDER_ICON=1 swift test --filter DocumentationRenderTests/testRenderAppIcon
